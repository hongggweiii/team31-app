"new_terms": the specific skills (or, rarely, specialisations) the text names or clearly relies on that the taxonomy does not cover, e.g. a named practice, standard, regulation-driven process or tool category.    - A new skill goes under an existing specialisation: give that specialisation in "specialisation".
